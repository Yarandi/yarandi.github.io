Why choose me

I help organizations replace manual processes, scattered spreadsheets, and fragile tools with software people actually enjoy using—so you can serve customers faster, gain real visibility into your operations, and scale without constant firefighting. My focus is on building practical, reliable systems that simplify how your business runs day to day.

With 13+ years of experience as a software engineer, I’ve built scalable web applications, backend systems, and real-time dashboards from the ground up. I specialize in Golang and PHP—developing APIs, microservices, and high-performance services—along with hands-on experience in AI/LLM integration and data-driven products. I prioritize clean architecture, maintainability, and solving complex technical challenges. Across industries like healthcare, hospitality, professional services, lending, and e-commerce, I’ve delivered end-to-end solutions for systems involving bookings, payments, compliance, and data. Beyond greenfield work, I support customer projects with this same skill set—embedded with your team or owning a technical slice so roadmaps and client commitments stay on track.
